# Prototype kit: first Sovereign Hoodie, no minimum

Decided 19 Oct 2026: no factory start order. Build the first hoodie from a premium blank plus a local tailor,
then make 5–15 more the same way as pre-orders come in. Ethan buys and pays for everything.

## 1. Buy (about $100–120)

| Item | What exactly | Where | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hoodie blank | Los Angeles Apparel **HF09GD**, 14 oz Heavy Fleece Hood, garment-dyed, **Black**, your size | losangelesapparel.net (no minimum) | ~$60–80 (check at checkout) |
| Silk | **22mm (22 momme) Charmeuse / Silk Crepe Satin, 55" wide, 1 yard** (enough for about 2 hoods) | dharmatrading.com | ~$31–34/yd + shipping |

**Silk colour decision:** Dharma's 22 momme comes in Ivory, Natural White, Champagne and Optic White. Black only comes in the
lighter 19.5 momme. Recommendation: **Ivory 22 momme**. It matches the "22-momme" claim on the site, and an ivory
lining inside a black hood is a strong visual. If you'd rather have black, buy black 19.5mm for the prototype only and
don't advertise "22 momme" for that piece.

Wash and dry the hoodie once before taking it to the tailor (it's garment-dyed, so it should barely move).

## 2. Tailor (about $60–120 for one)

Bring the hoodie, the silk and the printed **tailor-brief.pdf** (one page, in this folder). It says exactly how to build it and asks for prices at 1, 5 and 15 pieces.

Places to call (all from directory listings; call to confirm they do custom sewing, not just hems):

| Who | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Simply Barbara (tailor) | 31 Temple St, Quincy, MA 02169 | Closest to you; listed hours 10am–6pm |
| Brunhilde DRSMKR (dressmaker) | 72 Peterborough St, Boston, MA 02215 | Dressmakers are used to silk |
| Jordan the Tailor | Newbury St, Boston | Bridal/silk specialist, likely pricier |
| Thumbtack "clothing designers near Boston" | thumbtack.com | Freelance sewists who quote online |

**Phone script:** "Hi, I have a heavyweight cotton hoodie and want the hood lined with silk charmeuse. The silk sits inside
and attaches at the face opening and the neck seam. I have a one-page brief. Do you do that kind of custom work, and
roughly what would it cost for one?"

## 3. For 5–15 pieces later
Same route: buy blanks as pre-orders come in, and the same tailor lines them at the batch price. For a small sample
studio that can also handle embroidery and finishing, ask Good Clothing Company (Fall River, MA) for their
minimum. Embroidery and chrome prints can come from a local embroidery or print shop on the finished blank.

## 4. Timeline
- Day 1: order the hoodie + silk; call 2–3 tailors.
- Day 3–7: parts arrive; drop off with the tailor.
- About week 2: hoodie in hand to wear, wash-test and photograph (prompt P-01 in `docs/IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`).
