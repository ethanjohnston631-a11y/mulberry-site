# Image Prompts: What's Missing

The site's existing imagery prompts live in `assets/group-a-prompts.md`, and that file's **Global Brand Style** block
applies to everything below. These prompts fill the gaps that matter most for selling a silk-lined hoodie.

**How to use:** paste the prompt into your image generator (ChatGPT image generation, Midjourney v7, Gemini or Ideogram).
Generate 4 or more variations and pick the one with the cleanest fabric detail.

**Three rules that keep you legal and credible:**
1. Anything AI-generated that shows the product must be presented as a design render until real photos of the
   sample exist. The site already shows "Images show the design. Final production pieces may vary slightly" in
   waitlist and pre-order modes.
2. Replace renders with real photos of the physical sample as soon as you have one. The silk shot (P-01) matters most.
3. Leave the brand name off generated garments. Write `[BRAND]` as shown and add approved artwork in editing once
   the trademark question is settled (see `docs/LAUNCH-PLAN.md`, step 0).

## P-01: The Silk Reveal (most important image you don't have)
**Use:** product page hero detail, Instagram launch post, waitlist ads. **Ratio:** 4:5
```
Editorial product photograph. Close-up of the hood of a heavyweight washed black cotton hoodie, hood opening
pulled gently open by one hand to reveal a lustrous 22-momme mulberry silk charmeuse lining inside the hood.
The silk is [black / natural ivory]: liquid sheen, soft specular highlights, fine visible silk grain, no
wrinkles. A clean self-fabric cotton facing frames the hood opening, so the silk sits just inside the edge.
The cotton exterior is faded charcoal with a soft garment-washed texture and a few tiny white speckles.
Single warm champagne-gold side light from the left, deep ink-black shadows, generous negative space.
Shot on Hasselblad, 80mm, f/4, Kodak Portra 800 grain. Quiet luxury, no text, no logos, no people's faces.
```

## P-02: Silk vs Cotton Macro (explains the "why" without health claims)
**Use:** Why Silk page, carousel slide 2. **Ratio:** 1:1
```
Macro split-composition photograph. Left half: extreme close-up of heavyweight loopback French terry cotton,
matte, fibrous, slightly fuzzy texture. Right half: extreme close-up of mulberry silk charmeuse, smooth, liquid
reflective sheen, tight even weave. A single strand of dark hair lies across both halves, catching on the cotton
loops and lying smooth on the silk. Warm single side light, black background, shallow depth of field, analog film
grain. No text.
```

## P-07: Waitlist Campaign Graphic
**Use:** Instagram stories and posts announcing the waitlist. **Ratio:** 9:16
```
Minimal luxury campaign image. Pure ink-black background (#0b0b0c). In the lower third, a black heavyweight hoodie
lies folded, hood open, with a single beam of warm champagne-gold light falling across the silk-lined hood
```

Add the text afterwards in Canva: "THE FIRST RUN · Silk where it counts · Join the waitlist · link in bio".

## P-08: Brand Mark Exploration (only if you rebrand after the trademark review)
**Use:** exploring a distinctive, registrable mark. **Ratio:** 1:1
```
Minimal luxury fashion house monogram exploration sheet, 9 variations on an off-white background, black ink only.
Each mark combines a stylized silkworm or silk cocoon with an abstract initial. Geometric, engraved, heritage
feel, suitable for embroidery at 2 cm and for a woven label. Flat vector style, no gradients, no text other than
the initials.
```
AI-generated marks generally can't be protected by copyright. Have a designer redraw the chosen direction as
original vector artwork, and have the trademark attorney clear it before you use it.
