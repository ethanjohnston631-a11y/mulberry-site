MULBERRY EMPIRE|Construction & Build Sequence
Style MB-SOV-001 · The Sovereign Hoodie · v1.0: Development / Proto request · 02 Oct 2026 · Page 4

Seams & stitching (shell)

Body / sleeve seams4-thread overlock, 10–12 SPI
ArmholeOverlock + twin-needle coverstitch topstitch 0.6 cm
Hood CB seamOverlock + twin-needle coverstitch
Cuffs / hem ribOverlock, seam allowance pressed to body, single-needle topstitch
Kangaroo pocketHand openings faced, twin-needle 0.6 cm, bartacks at 4 corners
Back neckSelf-fabric tape 1.2 cm, twin-needle, covers neck/lining seam
Face openingSelf-fabric facing 2.5 cm, turned to inside, so silk never rolls to the outside

Silk lining

ConstructionTwo-panel hood, same pattern as shell minus 0.3 cm ease at crown, French seam at CB (no raw edges)
AttachmentJoined to shell at face opening under the facing, and at neckline enclosed by neck tape. Free-floating elsewhere.
Needle / SPIMicrotex 70/10, 14–16 SPI, light presser pressure, tissue/stabilizer as needed to avoid puckering
PressingSilk setting ≤ 150 °C with press cloth, no steam spotting

Required build sequence

  1. Cut shell, rib and silk. Shrinkage-test the shell first and add wash allowance to the shell pattern.
  2. Apply chest embroidery to the front panel (or post-wash; factory to advise from strike-off).
  3. Assemble the full shell (body, sleeves, rib, pocket, shell hood with facing), without the silk lining.
  4. Garment wash, sun-fade and speckle-spray the shell. Tumble dry or air dry to the agreed shrinkage.
  5. Apply HD/chrome prints after washing so the metallic finish stays crisp.
  6. Sew the silk lining hood separately. Attach it at the face facing and neckline, then close the back neck with the tape.
  7. Attach labels and hang tag. Final low-temperature press, protecting the silk and the prints.
  8. Inline + final QC (page 8), fold with tissue around the hood, and pack.

Wash & finish standard

  • Garment wash (enzyme or stone-free) for a soft, lived-in hand. Lightly sun-faded at shoulders, hood crown and pocket edge.
  • Fine white/off-white speckle spray, randomly scattered, approx. 15–30 dots per panel. No large splashes.
  • Light abrasion at hood edge and cuff/hem rib. No holes.
  • Approve a wash standard per colorway. Bulk must match the approved standard.

  3. Recycled clear polybag with suffocation warning, size sticker on the outside.
  4. Rigid or folding branded box (black, gold-foil logo), approx. 32 × 26 × 7 cm. TBC quote for both options.

Approval stages

1. Lab dipsShell ×5, rib ×5, silk lining
2. Strike-offsEmbroidery, front, back and sleeve prints, on washed fabric
3. Proto1 pc, size M, Black: construction and silk method
4. Fit sampleSize M on fit model, then measurements updated
5. PP samples1 per colorway, all trims and labels, wash standard
6. TOP sampleFrom bulk, before shipping

Quality requirements

  • Final inspection AQL 2.5 (major) / 4.0 (minor). Zero tolerance for holes, silk snags, silk visible on the outside, or print peeling.
  • Measurements within tolerance on 100% of the pieces measured.
  • Print adhesion and crack test after 5 home washes on the PP sample.
  • Color fastness to washing and rubbing ≥ grade 4 (shell and silk).
  • Silk lining: no puckering at seams, no shine marks from pressing, hood hangs without pulling.
  • Thread ends trimmed, no loose stitches, labels straight.

Open decisions (founder)

  • Drawcord: renders show none (recommended: keeps the silk hood clean, no eyelets through the lining). Alternative: flat cotton drawcord with metal aglets and eyelets in the shell only.
  • Silk lining color: one color for all colorways (black, or natural ivory as a reveal) keeps silk minimums low.
  • Front wordmark: final brand artwork pending trademark clearance.
  • Order quantity per color/size: to follow sample approval.
